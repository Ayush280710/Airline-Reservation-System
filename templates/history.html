The Horizon Story

Horizon Airlines is a fictional airline created for this school project.

2021 — The idea

The concept began in New Delhi with a goal of building a modern, accessible airline experience.

2022 — Horizon founded

The fictional founders built the airline around three principles: comfort, safety and simple digital booking.

2024 — International expansion

Horizon expanded its fictional network to destinations across Asia, Europe and the Middle East.

2026 — Digital Horizon

The current platform combines flight search, booking, passenger records, payments and support in one system.

All company history, founder details and network information on this page are fictional and created for demonstration.
